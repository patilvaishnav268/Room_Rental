# 🏡 UrbanNest Pune — Modern Room Rental & Flatshare Portal

**UrbanNest Pune** is a room rental web application featuring verified rooms across Pune's top residential and IT hubs. Built with fluid animations, glassmorphic UI, real-time filtering, interactive booking flows, and custom micro-interactions.

---

## 📍 Pune Localities Covered

- **Koregaon Park (KP)** — Leafy bohemian garden suites near Lane 7 & German Bakery
- **Baner** — High-rise executive smart pods near Balewadi High Street
- **Viman Nagar** — Cozy sunlit rooms minutes from Pune Airport & Phoenix Marketcity
- **Hinjawadi (Phase 1, 2, 3)** — Tech nomad studios with 100% DG power backup near Rajiv Gandhi IT Park
- **Kalyani Nagar** — Luxury riverfront penthouse suites overlooking Jogger's Park
- **Kothrud** — Cultural heritage rooms with balcony near Paud Road & Pune Metro
- **Aundh** — Nordic minimalist flatshares near Westend Mall & DP Road
- **Magarpatta City & Hadapsar** — Gated eco-township lofts near Cybercity
- **Kharadi** — High-rise riverside suites next to World Trade Center (WTC) & EON Free Zone
- **Wakad** — Modern budget-friendly studios near Mumbai-Pune Expressway & Dange Chowk
- **Bavdhan** — Scenic hillview villa suites near Chandani Chowk & NDA hills
- **Shivaji Nagar / FC Road** — Rooftop terrace rooms near Fergusson College & Deccan Gymkhana

---

## ✨ Features & Animations

1. **Fluid Ambient Animations**:
   - Floating gradient ambient orbs creating a depth effect.
   - Hero floating status pills (*"⭐ 4.98 Rating"*, *"⚡ Instant Book"*, *"🛡️ 100% Vetted"*) with smooth sine-wave bobbing.
   - Text shimmer gradients and subtle pulse effects.

2. **Interactive 3D Cards & Micro-Interactions**:
   - **3D Card Perspective Tilt**: Room cards tilt dynamically according to mouse cursor position.
   - **Multi-Image Carousel Slider**: Integrated photo slider on each card with smooth translation (`translateX`), active indicator dots, and hover arrow controls.
   - **Heart Wishlist Pop**: Explosive heart burst animation (`@keyframes heartBurst`) with floating badge counter update and slide-over **Saved Rooms Drawer**.

3. **Real-Time Pune Search & Reactive Filtering**:
   - Instant search across Pune localities, neighborhoods, landmarks, and amenities.
   - Locality dropdown filter (*Koregaon Park, Baner, Viman Nagar, Hinjawadi, etc.*).
   - Category chips (*Modern Studios, Private Rooms, Shared Flats, Penthouses, Scenic Villas*).
   - Real-time Price Range slider and live currency converter (**INR ₹ default**, USD $, EUR €, GBP £).
   - Sorting options (Recommended, Price Low-High, Price High-Low, Top Rated).

4. **Detailed Room Modal & Live Price Engine**:
   - Photo gallery with interactive thumbnail switcher.
   - Detailed specs (Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Sq Ft, Capacity, Host superhost status).
   - Dynamic price calculation reacting to check-in/checkout dates, cleaning fees, and service fees.

5. **Celebration Booking Engine**:
   - Custom **Canvas Confetti Particle System** with gravity and flutter physics.
   - Animated SVG checkmark drawing.
   - Reservation receipt card with unique booking code.

6. **"List Your Room" Multi-Step Wizard**:
   - 4-step wizard with animated progress dots.
   - Live photo preview.
   - Instantly publishes new rooms into the live grid.

7. **Theme & Extras**:
   - Dark Mode / Light Mode with smooth transition and icon animation.
   - Slide-in toast notification system.
   - Interactive FAQ accordion.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open a terminal in this directory:
   ```bash
   node server.js
   ```
2. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://localhost:3000
   ```
   *(Or simply double-click `index.html` in file explorer!)*
